Mini Cheatsheet (for revision)
• NSTEMI: clot partly blocks blood flow; ECG can show ST depression, T inversion, sometimes transient ST elevation.
• STEMI: artery totally blocked; ECG shows ST elevation pattern; territories include inferior, lateral, anterior/septal.
• Symptoms: crushing/pressure chest pain, diaphoresis, nausea/vomiting, dyspnea, anxiety.
